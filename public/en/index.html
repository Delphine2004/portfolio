Overview of my professional background

Junior Full-stack Web Developer with an atypical background: 15 years in the Parisian luxury hospitality industry have instilled in me a strong focus on user experience, managing complexity and attention to detail. I’ve traded customer service for code, while keeping the same obsession: operational excellence.

Looking back, this career change was obvious: I’ve always been the person connecting teams with technology, whether it was new management software or rethinking a customer journey.

Today, I bring this same rigor to technologies such as Symfony and JavaScript building solutions that are as robust as they are elegant.

Naturally curious and used to high responsibility environment, I am ready to put my energy into full-time, work-study, or freelance projects.
